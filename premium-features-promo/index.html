Frequently asked questions

What's included in the 14-day premium trial?

You get full access to all Premium features including Roadmaps, Flexible Hierarchy, advanced reporting, and unlimited users. No credit card required — just sign up and start exploring.

Will upgrading affect my existing projects and workflows?

Not at all. Premium features are additive — your current boards, backlogs, and workflows stay exactly as they are. You simply unlock new capabilities on top of what you already have.

How does Flexible Hierarchy work with my current issue types?

Flexible Hierarchy extends your existing issue types. You can add custom levels above or below your current structure — for example, adding Initiatives above Epics, or sub-tasks below Stories. Everything stays connected.

Do Roadmaps pull data from my existing boards automatically?

Yes! Roadmaps auto-sync with your boards and backlogs. Any issue you create or update is instantly reflected on the timeline — no duplicate data entry needed.

Can I cancel or downgrade after the trial?

Absolutely. You can downgrade back to Free at any time. Your data is always preserved — you just lose access to premium features until you upgrade again.

Is Premium suitable for small teams?

Premium is designed for teams of all sizes. Small teams benefit from Roadmaps to stay aligned without meetings, while larger organizations leverage Flexible Hierarchy to manage complexity across multiple projects.
